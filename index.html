The sound behind a world record.

Through Sound Hacker, Shariful Islam Ghuddy (composer & music producer) and MD Rizvi Rahman (music producer / pianist) created the jingle and background music for Clear Men's "Pass Koro, Record Goro" campaign (Unilever Bangladesh, agency Adcomm Limited) — used across the campaign videos, digital and on-ground activations. AboutBiography

Shariful Islam Ghuddy — also credited as Sharif Sumon — is a Bangladeshi music composer, sound engineer, mix & mastering engineer and sound designer based in Dhaka. He is the founder of the band Ghuddy, formed in 2007, where he plays guitar and flute. The band's name has since become part of his own, and he is widely known as "Ghuddy".

In 2012 he joined Radio Bhumi 92.8 FM as a sound engineer, and in 2015 he joined the radio station Colours FM 101.6 as a sound designer. In 2016 he advanced his studio-engineering training in Mumbai, studying sound engineering at Mumbai Music Institute and earning a professional certification from Steinberg. While in India he also performed as rhythm guitarist with the Indian band Reeva.

In 2018 he founded Sound Hacker, an audio production house and recording studio in Kakrail, Dhaka, where he works as composer, arranger and mix & mastering engineer alongside long-time collaborator Rizvi Rahman Adit. His work spans feature films, short films, television dramas, artist releases, jingles and advertising, NGO campaigns and dubbing — including music arrangement, mixing and mastering for Redoan Rony's feature film Domm (2026) and for seven songs of Tahsan Khan's Porcelaina Tahsan's Playlist (2025), among them "Prakritik", Tahsan's final released song.

Outside the studio, he is a passionate bike rider and traveler who loves exploring new places.

Band & studioGhuddy and Sound Hacker

Ghuddy

A Bangladeshi band formed in 2007, known for songs inspired by the Liberation War, the spirit of 1971 and the Bangla language. Shariful Islam Ghuddy is its founder, playing guitar and flute, alongside keyboardist Rizvi Rahman Adit.

  • Debut album Natai (2011)
  • Second album Manchitro (147570 Square Kilometers) — patriotic songs, launched March 2019 for Independence Day · Apple Music · Spotify
  • "Sharoni 71" — No. 1 Song of the Week on Airtel presents MTV Chart Attack (Season 1, Episode 12, 2014)

Sound Hacker

"Your sound, Hacked to perfection." A Dhaka-based audio production house founded in 2018 by Shariful Islam Ghuddy, bringing composition, recording, sound design, mixing and mastering into one workflow.
